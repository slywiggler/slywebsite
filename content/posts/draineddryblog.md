# [Drained Dry by Saranthos](https://archiveofourown.org/works/88368686)

Many would say that striking deals with powerful, desperate undead is not a path to a long life, or a very happy one for that matter. Mariella knows that, but when the lady of a not-so-abandoned manor offers you wealth, power, attention, and influence, how is she supposed to say no? Surely she can find a way to work around her new restrictions and get the better of her new dam.  
The world of Reverie is a pleasant distraction from everything else in Ella's life. Looming bills, a healthcare system that hates her, crushing dysphoria, they all fade away for the few hours of alone time she can eke out to plunge into the VR world. Now she's found herself at the beginning of a very interesting quest line, one that has her absolutely enthralled and curious to find the end of it.
